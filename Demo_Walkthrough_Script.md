# 1-to-2 Minute Video Demo Walkthrough Script
**Snapdeal AI Virtual KAM — Screen Recording Guide**

Follow this exact script when recording your screen for the assignment submission. It takes approximately **75 to 90 seconds** and hits every single grading criterion (two-way voice, data diagnosis, practical recommendations, and action plan summary).

---

### Pre-Recording Checklist (Takes 1 Minute)
1. Double-click `START_DEMO.bat` (or open `index.html` in **Google Chrome** / **Microsoft Edge**).
2. Unmute your computer microphone and turn up your speaker volume so the bot's voice is recorded clearly.
3. Open your screen recorder (Loom, OBS, or Windows Game Bar: press `Win + G`).
4. Set recording area to full screen or the browser window.

---

### Step-by-Step Recording Flow

#### Segment 1: Introduction & Context [0:00 – 0:15]
* **What's on Screen:** The full dashboard showing the Snapdeal Virtual KAM header, Rajesh Garments (Ludhiana) profile, and the metrics table.
* **What You Say:**
  > *"Hi Sourav and the Snapdeal team! This is my submission for the AI First Intern assignment: a voice-first AI Virtual Key Account Manager for Snapdeal sellers.*  
  > *Here we have Rajesh Garments from Ludhiana, whose monthly sales dropped by 32.5% from ₹2 Lakhs to ₹1.35 Lakhs. Let’s start a live voice call with Priya, our AI Virtual KAM."*

---

#### Segment 2: Starting the Voice Call & The Problem [0:15 – 0:40]
* **What You Do:** Click the blue **"Start Voice Call"** button (or click the first quick chip: `📉 My sales dropped significantly this month...`).
* **Bot Speaks (Priya):**
  > *"Namaste Rajesh ji! I'm Priya, your Snapdeal AI Virtual Key Account Manager. I noticed you called regarding your store performance. How can I help you grow today?"*
* **What You Say into Microphone** *(or click chip)*:
  > *"My sales have dropped significantly this month. I don’t understand why. Can you help me?"*
* **What's on Screen:** Point out how the visualizer orb pulses blue while listening, and animates red soundwaves when Priya speaks.
* **Bot Responds (Priya):**
  > *"Rajesh ji, I completely understand your concern. Losing ₹65,000 in monthly revenue is tough, but looking at your telemetry, your customer demand is actually healthy. Your Average Selling Price held steady at ₹500! The real revenue leak is that your Out-of-Stock SKUs surged from 5 to 18, and your cancellations shot up to 14%. When top products go out of stock, Snapdeal's algorithm suppresses your entire store's visibility. Are you currently facing supplier delays from your fabric mills?"*

---

#### Segment 3: Root Cause & Empathic Probing [0:40 – 1:05]
* **What You Say into Microphone** *(or click chip)*:
  > *"My supplier delayed raw materials so 13 items ran out of stock. What should I do first?"*
* **What's on Screen:** Hover your mouse over the **Out of Stock (18 SKUs)** and **Cancellation Rate (14%)** cards. Point out how the metric cards highlight dynamically.
* **Bot Responds (Priya):**
  > *"I completely understand—supplier delays in apparel happen all the time. But here is the smart strategy: don't wait to restock all 18 items at once. Focus 100% of your supplier pressure on your Top 5 'Hero' SKUs that brought 60% of your ₹2 Lakhs last month. Second, let's activate a 5-unit buffer in Seller Central so zero out-of-stock orders occur. And third, while we wait for new fabric, we can run a Snapdeal Flash Promo on your 32 currently in-stock items to revive daily cash flow."*

---

#### Segment 4: Action Plan & Closing the Loop [1:05 – 1:30]
* **What You Say into Microphone** *(or click chip)*:
  > *"Please summarize the key issues and give me my 3 action steps."*
* **What's on Screen:** The action plan card updates its badge to **"KAM Approved"**.
* **Bot Responds (Priya):**
  > *"Here is your 3-step Snapdeal Turnaround Plan, Rajesh ji: First, restock your Top 5 Hero SKUs within 48 hours to recover roughly ₹50,000 in lost orders. Second, set a 5-unit inventory safety buffer in Seller Central right now to eliminate phantom orders and push cancellations back below 8%. Third, enroll your 32 active in-stock listings in this weekend's Snapdeal Payday Fashion Carnival with a ₹40 voucher. I've sent this complete action memo to your dashboard. Shall I set a follow-up call for this Thursday at 4 PM to review progress?"*
* **What You Do:** Click the **"Copy WhatsApp Brief"** button (shows a popup toast: *"WhatsApp summary copied to clipboard"*), then point to the **"Export KAM Memo"** button.
* **What You Conclude (Spoken):**
  > *"As you can see, this bot doesn't just give generic advice—it caught that pricing wasn't the issue, diagnosed the stockouts, and generated an instant action plan with a 1-click WhatsApp memo. Thank you, and I look forward to the live demonstration!"*

---

### Pro Tips for a Perfect Recording:
- If you feel nervous speaking into the microphone, you can simply click the **Quick Test Scenario Chips** below the call button! The bot will process them as speech and reply out loud in natural voice.
- Keep your mouse movements smooth and confident.
- Upload your recording to **Loom**, **Google Drive** (ensure link sharing is set to *"Anyone with the link can view"*), or **YouTube** (Unlisted).
